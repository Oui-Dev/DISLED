# Board F1B9ABCD5A9BBD15E339E339

First v4 board (silkscreen DISLED Rev 1.0), brought up on 2026-10-19 and
2026-10-20 on the Windows PC, through an earthed monitor hub. The design
changes this bring-up led to are in
[ADR 0009](../decisions/0009-bring-up-corrections.md); the tuned values are
in `firmware/src/app/app_config.c`.

Enclosure: PLA, closed for the touch and color tests. Touch electrode: a
ring of 30 mm outside and 20 mm inside diameter, under 0.8 mm of PLA (the
center stays open for the light), wired with a JST lead along the wall.

## Faults found and fixed

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Reset loop every 2 s, no USB; the diagnostic build blinked 9 times | the CPU never woke from WFI | no WFI in the main loop |
| Light sensor absent | SDK `I2C_Init` wrote PCLK1 = 72 into a 6-bit field (became 8) | PCLK1 = 36 MHz |
| Light sensor reads never completed | STM32-style two-byte read (POS, BTF) | WCH byte-by-byte read |
| First transfer after a bus recovery lost arbitration | the recovery's STOP was a START (SDA fell with SCL high) | clean STOP; recovery at start-up, after failures, before each probe |
| TouchKey saturated near 4093 | WCH example timing (charge 16, 7.5 cycles) | charge 3, discharge 8, ADC sample 1.5 cycles |
| Light taps merged, center of the lid stopped working | fixed thresholds; baseline pulled down by a hand near the lid | floor-relative detector, baseline frozen in the proximity band |
| Warm white yellow at 10 %, pink then yellow at 3 % | blue pulse of 1 tick (14 ns) gave no light | pulse loss per channel, 1/16-tick dithering |

## LED and colors

Judged through the closed enclosure.

| Item | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Green | 0 / 65535 / 0 | good |
| Orange | 65535 / 29000 / 0 | 16384 of green looked almost red |
| Red | 65535 / 0 / 0 | good |
| Warm white | 63000 / 58000 / 1600 | the first guess was clearly pink |
| Pulse loss R / G / B | 2 / 3 / 4 3⁄16 ticks (28 / 42 / 58 ns) | warm white keeps its hue from 3 to 100 %, a very slight tint left at 3 % |
| Brightness | 100 % manual is enough for daylight | low end of the automatic curve raised × 1.5 (too dim through the PLA) |

The orange came closer to the red once the pulse loss was in, but stays
clearly distinct at 3 and 10 %.

## Light sensor

- ID register `0xC481`, configuration `0x1000` read back, 60 reads out of
  60 without error after the I2C fixes.
- The lux follows a flashlight on the left window; the sensor faces the
  left edge as designed.
- The automatic curve needs tuning (phase 2): note the lux shown by the
  app and the level wanted in several situations, then fit the curve.

## Touch

TouchKey at rest: about 2620 counts. Values are the drop below the
baseline after the 20 ms average, in per mille.

| Situation | Drop (‰) |
|---|---|
| Idle, LED on | 0 to 4 |
| Idle, LED off (LED switching shifts the reading) | 3 to 7 |
| Hand approaching, 1 cm or closer | up to 27 |
| Hand resting near the lid between taps | 20 to 30 |
| Finger on the center of the lid (inside the ring) | 45 to 55 |
| Light finger on the center | 38 to 45 |
| Finger on the electrode ring | 98 to 105 |
| Finger on the center, other hand on the USB plug | 64 to 91 |
| Object on the lid | about 100 |

- The center gives half the signal of the ring. If the Mac shows missed
  touches there, shrink the inner diameter (for example to 15–16 mm),
  which costs some of the lit area.
- Holding the board or the USB plug ties the body to the board ground and
  doubles the drop: test with one hand only.
- Gestures: short presses step through green, orange, red; long presses
  turn the lamp off and on; a short press while off is ignored. With the
  final settings, 25 of 25 recorded taps were found, including light taps
  with the hand resting near the lid, with no skipped color and no false
  long press.
- Object on the lid: it turns the lamp off at 2 s (taken for a long press),
  is absorbed at 10 s, and its removal fires nothing. Kept as is (the
  other option, undoing the long press once the object is recognized, was
  turned down).
- The touch noise did not change when the PWM dithering interrupt started
  running every period.

## USB and app

- Debug build: `1209:0002`, HID and CDC (`COM3`). Release build:
  `1209:0001`, HID only, no serial port.
- Serial number = chip UID, as above.
- Desktop app: connection, controls, live telemetry and reconnection after
  an unplug all work with both builds. After a flash it reconnects too, but
  only after several seconds (the board goes through the bootloader first).

## Phase 2 checks (2026-10-21)

Firmware `75a4799` (debug build), Windows PC.

### Bootloader and low power

- Software entry into the ROM bootloader: impossible on this chip, see
  [ADR 0010](../decisions/0010-firmware-updates-from-the-app.md). The
  system flash (`0x1FFF8000`, about 15 KB of code) was read through the
  console and disassembled to find out; the dump stays out of the
  repository (WCH code).
- WFI: works in a tight loop, hangs in the main loop; low power without
  WFI, see [ADR 0011](../decisions/0011-low-power.md).
- USB suspend (PC sleep, then wake): LED off at once, lamp back in its
  previous state (orange), one suspend counted, no reset. The longest task
  run during suspend (1 MHz clock): 1.7 ms.

### Diagnostics and timing

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Stack never used (of 2048 B) | 1320 to 1376 B |
| Longest task run, full speed | USB 41 us, touch 7 us, light sensor 490 us (I2C read), output 6 us, host 27 us, console 160 us |
| Watchdog | 1 s: more than 500 times the longest task |
| Resets counted while WFI hung the loop | 89 watchdog resets in a row, then none with the fix |

### Self-test and bias

- Self-test from the console, twice: result `0x0f` (light sensor, idle
  touch, touch detected, completed) in 8 to 9 s. Colors checked by eye.
  First run: the white step looked pink (equal channels) and the release of
  the finger stepped the color; both fixed (warm white step, consumed
  touch), the second run left the lamp as it was.
- Automatic brightness bias: applied level 40 per mille at 0 %, 52 at
  +30 %, 28 at −30 %.

### Touch noise against the PWM sample phase (review N1)

Touch reading, 20 ms averages over 3 s (about 160 values) for each case.
Standard deviation in per mille of the reading (about 2618 counts).

| LED | Phase 100 | Phase 1800 (default) | Phase 3000 | Random phase |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Off | 2.31 | 2.14 | 2.17 | 2.05 |
| Warm white 50 % (26 mA) | 2.15 | 2.35 | 2.29 | 2.20 |
| Warm white 100 % (125 mA) | 2.05 | 2.36 | 2.43 | 2.09 |

No coupling of the LED switching into the touch is measurable: the noise
and the mean reading stay the same whatever the phase and the LED current.
The synchronized sampling stays (it costs nothing).
